# HKJSTU Public Website — Content Plan

## Purpose

Create a clear, welcoming introduction to the Hong Kong Joint School Technology Union (HKJSTU) for secondary school students, member schools, teachers, and potential partners.

The website should answer four questions quickly:

1. What is HKJSTU?
2. What can students take part in?
3. Who is the community for?
4. How can someone join or collaborate?

**Recommended format:** a single-page introduction website with a simple navigation bar. Programme details, the full team, and application forms can become separate pages later.

**Tone:** energetic, credible, inclusive, and student-led. Use plain English and avoid overly formal constitutional language.

## 3. About HKJSTU

**Section heading:**

> One community across schools

**Draft copy:**

> HKJSTU brings together secondary school students from local and international schools across Hong Kong. Led by students, the Union creates opportunities to explore technology beyond the classroom—from coding and cybersecurity to digital design, engineering, and aviation technology.
>
> Our mission is to bridge gaps between schools, empower young people, and create positive social impact through technology. We do this by connecting students with one another and with institutions, industry organisations, and new learning opportunities.

**Three short value statements:**

- **Collaborate across schools** — Meet students with different interests, experiences, and ideas.
- **Build practical skills** — Learn through competitions, workshops, seminars, and hands-on projects.
- **Turn ideas into impact** — Explore how technology can respond to real needs and benefit society.

**Optional community fact:**

> 19 member schools in the 2026–2027 school network

Only publish this number after confirming that the supplied member-school list is current.

## 6. Membership

**Section heading:**

> Find your place in Hong Kong’s student technology community

**Draft copy:**

> Membership is free and open to current secondary school students in Hong Kong who are interested in technology, digital design, software development, engineering, or related fields. You do not need to be an expert—curiosity and a willingness to take part are enough.

**Member benefits:**

- Take part in Union activities and community events
- Access selected members-only workshops, networking sessions, and programmes
- Receive priority for selected high-demand events according to membership tier
- Connect with students from other schools and develop through projects and leadership opportunities

**How to join:**

1. Complete the official membership application form.
2. Wait for confirmation from the Executive Committee.
3. Start taking part in the HKJSTU community.

**Call to action:**

> **Apply for membership** — `[Official application form link]`

**Optional short explanation of progression:**

> General Members can progress to Full Member status through sustained participation and contribution. Fellow Membership recognises service as a School Representative or Executive Committee member.

**Community values:**

> Members are expected to contribute to an inclusive and respectful community and uphold academic and technical integrity.

## Content Deliberately Left Out of the Introduction Website

The following source material is useful for internal governance but would distract from a concise public introduction:

- Detailed meeting, voting, appointment, and amendment procedures
- Disciplinary procedures and appeal mechanisms
- Financial administration and dissolution clauses
- Detailed intellectual-property licensing terms
- Full descriptions of every committee role

Membership terms, privacy information, and relevant participation rules should instead be available through clearly linked policy documents or the application process.
